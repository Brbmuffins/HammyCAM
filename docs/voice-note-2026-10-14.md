## What it asks for

1. **Smooth, professional motion is the #1 priority.** The current setup is web-based, jittery, with a poor
   frame rate. Current 3D models feel rigid.
2. **Goofy object avatars that bend.** For example, a sunflower in a pot: the face sits on the flower, the pot
   stays rooted, the stem is fluid and stretches as you stretch. Or a T-bone steak: the base stays still, and
   lowering your face scrunches or folds the steak like it's hunching over.
3. **Quick creation:** "What do you want?" "A steak." It gets made goofy, rigged automatically and added.
   Wants to work with STL files too, and expects the models to need skeletons.
4. **Face options as quick toggles:**
   - *My face*: the streamer's real face on the object.
   - *Digital face*: cartoon eyes and mouth that read facial expressions and mouth shapes ("ooh", "ah",
     showing teeth).
   - Also 2D object vs 3D object.
5. **A built-in library** to scroll through: "here are the 10 rigged 3D objects we have, more to come".
6. **Chat changing what you are:** liked the idea and thinks it can be implemented.
7. **Made to be sold.** The aim is to package and sell it; the speaker believes nothing on the market does
   exactly this.
8. The speaker asked to be told if this is too ambitious.
